Hollywood HVAC work centers on a specific challenge: the housing stock is dominated by buildings constructed before central AC existed. The famous courtyard apartments along Franklin, the Spanish Revival fourplexes off Highland, the 1930s art-deco residential buildings around Cahuenga — none of them were designed with cooling in mind. Modern Hollywood HVAC is almost entirely retrofit work, and retrofit work in 90-year-old buildings has its own rules.

## What's Specific About HVAC in Hollywood

**Pre-war housing stock.** Hollywood's residential identity was built between 1915 and 1940. The apartment buildings, courtyard fourplexes, and bungalow courts that define the neighborhood were constructed with steam or hot water heating, plaster walls, and no AC infrastructure of any kind. Original wall thicknesses, transom windows, and high ceilings provided passive cooling adequate for 1925 — but not for 2026 summers.

**Steam and hot water heating systems.** Many Hollywood apartment buildings still operate on the original boiler-fed steam or hydronic heating systems. These are reliable, character-appropriate, and incompatible with adding integrated cooling. The result: heat from the building, cooling from window units or aftermarket mini splits.

**Hollywood Hills additions.** Hillside Hollywood homes have a typical pattern: an original 1920s or 1930s core, with additions and remodels added every 10–20 years since. Each addition typically came with its own HVAC zone or its own window AC, never properly integrated with the original. We see hillside Hollywood homes with three different cooling systems serving three different parts of the same house.

**Mini split as standard solution.** For Hollywood apartments, condos in pre-war buildings, and renovated bungalows, [ductless mini split installation](/services/heat-pump-services-los-angeles) is the dominant solution. We install more mini split systems in Hollywood than in any other LA neighborhood, and the install patterns are well-established for the building types here.

## Hollywood Microclimate

Hollywood proper sits in a thermal pocket between the Santa Monica Mountains and the LA basin. Summer afternoon highs typically run 5–10°F warmer than coastal areas but cooler than the San Fernando Valley. The residential streets south of Sunset run measurably warmer than the immediate hill base — the canyon ventilation effect that cools the hills doesn't reach the flats below.

Hollywood Hills properties (north of Sunset, into the canyons) experience cooler summer afternoons but greater day-night temperature swings — relevant for system sizing on hillside homes.

## Common Hollywood Service Issues

- **Mini split outdoor unit placement.** Tight side yards, courtyard buildings, and shared walls limit installation locations. We pre-evaluate every Hollywood mini split job before quoting.
- **Window AC replacement and upgrade.** Many Hollywood apartments still rely on window units. Upgrades to mini split systems require both equipment cost and building approval. We handle the documentation.
- **Old hydronic heating compatibility.** Owners who want to add cooling without disrupting original steam or hot water heat need ductless solutions — adding a heat pump that overlays the existing heating system works in some configurations.
- **Hillside duct repair.** Older hillside homes with attic ductwork frequently have damage from settling, additions, and animal activity. [Duct sealing and repair](/services/duct-repair-los-angeles) recovers significant cooling capacity.
- **Variable cooling needs across multi-unit buildings.** Top-floor apartments are dramatically warmer than ground-floor units in pre-war buildings without insulation.
