Carrier is one of the most widely installed premium AC brands in Los Angeles, particularly in mid-to-high-end homes built after 1990. When a Carrier stops cooling, homeowners often assume the worst — especially given what a Carrier system costs to install.

## Why Carrier ACs Stop Cooling in Los Angeles

- **Dirty condenser coil from LA air quality** — smog and dust accumulate on the outdoor coil and reduce its ability to reject heat; this is a maintenance issue, not a Carrier-specific defect
- **Low refrigerant charge** — Carrier systems lose refrigerant only through leaks; a low reading means a leak needs to be located and repaired, not just topped off
- **Failed [run capacitor](/cost/ac-capacitor-replacement-cost-los-angeles)** — gives the compressor and [fan motor](/cost/ac-fan-motor-replacement-cost-los-angeles) the electrical jolt to start; when it fails, the unit hums but does not cool
- **Thermostat or control board issue** — a misconfigured or faulty thermostat can prevent the system from responding to cooling calls even when the home is warm
- **[Refrigerant leak](/cost/refrigerant-leak-repair-cost-los-angeles) at the evaporator coil or line set** — Carrier Infinity and Performance series coils can develop pinhole leaks over time; in LA's coastal areas, salt air from the ocean accelerates this process

## Safe Checks Before You Call

1. **Thermostat:** Set to COOL, set point below current room temperature, batteries fresh if battery-powered.
2. **Air filter:** A dirty filter is the most frequently overlooked cause of Carrier cooling failure; replace if it has not been changed in 60+ days.
3. **Circuit breaker:** The outdoor unit and air handler are on separate breakers — check both.
4. **Outdoor unit:** Is the condenser fan spinning? Are the refrigerant lines frosted? Frost on the lines means restricted airflow or low refrigerant — turn the system off and call.

Carrier warranty terms require refrigerant work to be performed by a licensed contractor. DIY refrigerant handling voids the Carrier manufacturer warranty.

## Carrier in Los Angeles — What You Should Know

For in-warranty repairs, using a Carrier Factory Authorized Dealer may be required to maintain warranty coverage on certain claim types. Confirm Factory Authorized status with any company you call before approving warranty work.

For standard repairs on out-of-warranty Carrier systems, any C-20 licensed technician who carries Carrier-compatible parts can do the work. You do not need an authorized dealer for non-warranty repairs.

Carrier Infinity systems — the high-efficiency variable-speed line common in newer LA homes — use a communicating control system that requires a technician experienced with the Infinity controller. Not every HVAC company in Los Angeles has this experience. When calling for Infinity system service, confirm upfront that the technician has worked on Carrier's communicating systems specifically.

**Note on Bryant:** Bryant is a sister brand to Carrier — same equipment, same factories, different nameplate. Everything on this page applies equally to Bryant systems.

## When to Repair vs Replace Your Carrier AC in Los Angeles

**Under 10 years old:** Repair. Carrier systems are built for 15–18 years with maintenance. A $400 repair on a 7-year-old Carrier is a sound investment.

**10–16 years old:** Run the numbers. Get the repair quote and a replacement quote from the same visit. If the repair is under 40% of replacement cost and the compressor is healthy, repair typically makes sense.

**Over 16 years with a major failure:** Modern Carrier systems are significantly more efficient than 2008-era equipment. In Los Angeles, where central AC runs 5–8 months per year depending on location — shorter on the coast, longer in the inland valleys — the utility savings from a higher-efficiency replacement accumulate faster than in most other markets. At this age and failure level, get both numbers and compare honestly.
